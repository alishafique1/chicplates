# ChicPlates Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview

**ChicPlates** is a Gen Z car accessories brand focused on style, self-expression, and aesthetic customization.

### Brand Voice
- Fun & playful
- Confident
- Trendy but accessible
- Authentic

### Target Audience
- Gen Z (18-26)
- First-time car owners
- Style-conscious
- Social media native

## Content Guidelines

### Photography Style
- Bright, natural lighting
- Lifestyle-focused (car interiors, driving scenes)
- User-generated content aesthetic
- Close-up product shots on clean backgrounds

### Video Style
- Quick cuts (TikTok native)
- Trending audio
- Before/after reveals
- POV installations

### Caption Voice
- Use "you/your" language
- Emoji-friendly (but not overboard)
- Include CTA
- Hashtag strategy: 5-10 per post
