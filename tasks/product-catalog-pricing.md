## 6. Business Model Comparison

| Path | Frames | Accessories | Blended Margin | Best For |
|------|--------|-------------|----------------|----------|
| **A) Full CJ Dropship** | CJ sourcing | CJ | 50–65% | Zero risk, fast launch |
| **B) Hybrid ⭐ RECOMMENDED** | In-house / Alibaba bulk | CJ dropship | 60–70% | Quality on hero product |
| **C) Alibaba Bulk Branded** | 500 MOQ, your logo | CJ dropship | 65–75% | Scaling, true brand |

### Recommended rollout
1. **Now:** Path B — keep frames in-house (premium unboxing = differentiator), dropship accessories via CJ with $0.30 branded stickers.
2. **At ~50+ frames/mo:** move to Path C — order 500 branded frames from Alibaba to lock in 75% margins.
